\section{Model settings}\label{app:models}

All models use the \nFeatures{} inputs listed in \cref{sec:models} and the same split: listings are
grouped by building, and 20\% of buildings are held out for testing (scikit-learn's
\texttt{GroupShuffleSplit}, seed 710).

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em, labelindent=0pt, font=\normalfont\bfseries]
  \item[Naive baseline] the median asking rent of the training listings in the same market and
  bedroom count.
  \item[Linear regression] ordinary least squares, after median imputation of missing values.
  \item[Decision tree] maximum depth 10, at least 20 listings per leaf, after median imputation.
  \item[Gradient boosting] histogram-based gradient boosting with 100 iterations, at most 31 leaves
  per tree, and a learning rate of 0.1; missing values are handled natively.
\end{description}
